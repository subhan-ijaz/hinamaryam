03 Design Process

BEHIND THE SCENES!

"How ideas evolve from rough sketches into clean, interactive user interfaces."

01 / Wireframing

BACK TO PAPER

Wireframing user flows on paper before touching pixels—mapping layout structure, navigation clarity, and content hierarchy first.

02 / Visual Systems

THINKING OUT LOUD

Structuring typography, color theory, and component systems so every screen maintains strong visual consistency and usability standards.

03 / Prototyping

COFFEE IN HAND

Building responsive Figma prototypes and parallax interactions—testing breakpoints and micro-transitions until every click feels effortless.
